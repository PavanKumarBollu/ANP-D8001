Problem-Based Learning: “Decode the AI Student”:



**Your role**



&#x09;You are a Data Analyst Intern at a college-tech startup.





The startup has collected data from **50,000** students to understand:



&#x09;“How are Gen-Z students actually using Generative AI, and what does their AI behavior tell us?”





Your manager doesn't want a simple report.



&#x09;They want you to clean, transform, decode, and create useful text-based labels from the raw student data using Excel **Text Functions.**



**The Mission** 



Your manager sends you this message:



“We have 50,000 students, but the raw data is boring. I want you to transform these records into something our Gen-Z marketing team can actually understand.”



Your job is to create a new worksheet called:



&#x09;AI Student Decoder



You will take the original data and create useful text-based information from it.
